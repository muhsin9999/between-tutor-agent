What it does

The tutor sends one line at the end of a lesson. That is the only thing she types all week. The agent plans the six days, works them with the student in plain Telegram chat, and five minutes before the next lesson builds her a briefing panel whose shape comes from what the week actually produced.

The tutor · once a week

One line in

Jonas — German past tense of irregular verbs, ten minutes a day. He's nervous about speaking out loud.

Six days come out of that. The nervousness lands as a produce task on day 5, not day 1.

The student · every day

Plain chat, forever

One question at a time, on a bus, in the app he already has. No install, no account, no password. Eight choices or fewer become an inline keyboard — never a screen to open.

The tutor · five minutes before

A panel nobody drew

A model picks which of seven typed components appear, in what order, holding what contents. A quiet week and an error week produce genuinely different screens from the same code.

Two briefs, one renderer. The cut between them is the argument — one week alone proves nothing.

Three rules that are the product

01

The student never gets a panel. Plain chat, forever. The premise is that he will not open a practice app — that is why the six days are empty. A panel on his side would rebuild the exact failure the product exists to fix.

02

The revision is never cut. It is the only thing separating this from a scheduled-message product. Without it the honest description is “practice scheduler”, not “agent”.

03

The model composes; it does not write code. It chooses which of seven typed components appear, in what order, holding what contents. Nothing it emits is executed, and an unknown component fails validation rather than rendering blank.

How it's built

apps/channelThe Telegram process. CopilotKit Channels on the direct adapter, long-polling over grammY — no webhook, no public URL.
apps/webThe tutor's Mini App panel and its API. Next.js, CopilotKit generative UI, deliberately without the chat sidebar — a chat sidebar inside a chat app would be absurd.
agent-corePlanning, revision, composition, the evidence triggers, and the frozen contracts both sides build against.
contracts.tsOne file holding the plan shape and the component vocabulary. The model's output schema and the renderer's types are the same declaration.
.data/between.jsonSession state on disk. Not a database, on purpose: deleting one file gives a clean take.
ModelOpenAI gpt-4.1-mini through the Vercel AI SDK, with structured outputs. Provider swaps in one environment variable.

What it deliberately isn't

Not a curriculum, a course platform, or a replacement teacher. It works only on what the human tutor assigned and introduces no new material. It reports to nobody but that student's own tutor. It stops rather than escalating when someone goes quiet. It does not message under-18s except through a parent's account.
